    AI analysis

    AegisAI (AGIS) is an early-stage Solana token with a fixed 10B max supply: 85% is reserved for mining, 10% team, 5% ecosystem. Miners earn by power × time and by checking encrypted real-world-asset (RWA) jobs; emission halves about every 2 years and difficulty rises with miner count. Today the browser miner and wallet-signed claims work; on-chain payout runs through a claim server with daily caps, and the public RWA job queue is still local to each browser. The supply above is live on-chain — the strongest honest signal of real mining activity. Treat AGIS as high-risk and experimental. Not financial advice.

    Mined = on-chain supply − team/eco tokens already minted. Burned = highest supply seen − current supply (this browser). Charts fill up as you keep the page open.
