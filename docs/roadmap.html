Roadmap

What works, what we are building, and what comes later. We update this page as things ship.

Now — works today, on-chain only

  • Seal on Solana · live
    Wallet-first: the fingerprint is made in your browser, you sign and pay ~$0.001 straight to Solana. The record includes an on-chain fingerprint index. No AegisAI server.
  • Verify from Solana · live
    Drop the file or paste the tx — the browser checks Solana directly and shows who signed (your wallet or the firm key).
  • Receipt: download, print, QR · live
    Your receipt (fingerprint, tx, time) is saved by you. Anyone can check it on Solana.
  • Burn AGIS · live
    Real SPL burn from your own wallet on Burn/Stake. Supply goes down on-chain.

Beta — still uses our test server

  • Mining rewards · beta · server
    Miner grading and claims still run through our beta test server. They move to the Solana program below; until then rewards may change.

Next — building

  • On-chain Solana program: stake, unstake, mining rewards · building
    Replaces the server and the firm vault: time-locked stake you withdraw yourself, and reward emission enforced by the program. Audit before mainnet.
  • Encrypted file copy on Arweave / IPFS · planned
    Optional: your file is encrypted in your browser and only you hold the key. The encrypted copy goes to Arweave/IPFS and its hash + ID into the Solana record. We never hold keys or files.
  • AGIS/SOL liquidity pool · after funding
    Needed before any payment in AGIS. No pool exists today.

AGIS for easier sealing — coming

  • Pay the seal fee in AGIS · after LP
    Your wallet swaps a little AGIS to SOL via Jupiter for the network fee in the same transaction; part can be burned.
  • Seal credits · after program
    Holding or staking AGIS gives prepaid or discounted seals, counted by the on-chain program — not by our server.
  • Gasless (sponsored) seals for AGIS holders · after program
    A fee-payer co-signs and pays the SOL fee; the program charges your AGIS credits. No SOL needed in your wallet.

Later

  • Mobile apps · later
    Same Solana record format — apps talk to Solana directly.
  • QTSP / notary partner · later
    Qualified signatures only through a licensed partner — we will not imitate them.

Honest notes: seals prove a file fingerprint at a time — not legal title, not a notary or eIDAS stamp. Our servers store nothing for seals.
