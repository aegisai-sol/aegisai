The shield for AI truth.

AegisAI is a decentralized network that fact-checks AI output. Instead of burning power on meaningless hashes, any machine — even old, weak ones — earns $AGIS by running useful AI micro-verification tasks.

What

A permissionless verification layer for AI. Claims produced by language models are split into small, checkable units and distributed to a network of independent nodes. Their combined judgment becomes a verifiable, on-chain fact record.

Why

AI hallucinates — confidently. Traditional mining wastes energy on puzzles with no value. AegisAI redirects compute toward something the world actually needs: trustworthy AI answers, rewarded fairly, accessible to hardware that would otherwise sit idle.

How it works

01

Micro-tasks

AI output is broken into atomic claims and dispatched as lightweight verification jobs any CPU can handle.

02

Consensus

Multiple independent nodes check each claim. Agreement is weighted by reputation; dishonest nodes lose standing.

03

On-chain fact

The verdict is anchored on Solana as a compact, auditable proof. Verifiers are rewarded in $AGIS.

// Proof-of-Verification (concept)
claim   = split(ai_output)
votes   = nodes.verify(claim)        // k-of-n
verdict = consensus(votes, reputation)
solana.anchor(hash(claim), verdict)  // → reward $AGIS
