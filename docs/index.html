    How it works

    From a hallucinated answer to a verifiable receipt

    AI invents facts and fake citations; checking them by hand is slow and costly; billions of weak devices sit idle. PoV-R turns that idle compute into verification.

    1. 1 · AI answer
    2. 2 · Micro-claims
    3. 3 · k nodes verify
    4. 4 · Commit / reveal
    5. 5 · Receipt + rewards
    Practical example
    AI answer

    “Bulgaria joined the EU in 2007 [source: europa.eu/…]”

    Micro-claim

    claim#2: year(join_EU) == 2007 · cite = europa.eu page

    Nodes fetch the cited page (Proof-of-Source)

    Phones, laptops, Pis check independently. Page text says 2004.

    Commit → reveal

    Votes hidden until window closes: 4/5 FALSE. Canary traps catch lazy nodes.

    Receipt

    verdict: FALSE · cite mismatch · digest anchored on Solana

    Honest nodes earn: 70% verifiers · 20% Reservoir · 5% treasury · 5% burn.

    Not PoW

    No hash waste — every unit of work checks a real claim.

    Not just PoS

    Stake alone earns nothing; useful, correct verification does.

    Not a central fact-checker

    k independent nodes + commit/reveal; anyone can audit the receipt.

    Two sides of one network

    Supply: anyone with a device. Demand: anyone shipping AI.

    Verification market

    For builders & enterprises: send AI output, receive per-claim verdicts plus an on-chain receipt your users and auditors can check. (Verification API — Phase 4.)

    Weak-machine mining

    For node operators: a phone in a browser, a laptop, a Raspberry Pi or a server. No GPU, no hash waste — every vote reduces AI error.

    Reputation staking

    Stake AGIS to weight your votes; accuracy history is public and on-chain.

    Not PoW
