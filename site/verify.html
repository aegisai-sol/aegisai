    Receipt anatomy: quorum + fee split DEMO

    Several independent nodes fetch the source. A receipt is VALID when ≥ quorum nodes agree on content hash and verdict, VALID_VERDICT when verdicts agree but the page changed between fetches (ads/timestamps), HASH_ONLY / NO_CONSENSUS otherwise. The verification fee then splits 70% miners · 20% Reservoir · 5% treasury · 5% burn. If the calibration canary fails, the miners' share goes to the Reservoir.

Redirects are followed manually (max 5 hops, SSRF check per hop): https→http downgrades are refused, cross-site redirects are refused, and http→https upgrades (or host changes within the same site) are flagged in the receipt with the full redirect_chain and final_url.
