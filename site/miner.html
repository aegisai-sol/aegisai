How rewards work

  • 10B max · 85% mining · 10% team · 5% ecosystem.
  • Power × time: Phone ×1 · Laptop ×2.5 · Server ×6. More time online = more AGIS.
  • Difficulty grows with miners, so supply stays on schedule.
  • Halving every ~2 years.
  • Bigger RWA = more AGIS.
unit   = 0.006738 AGIS × tier / 2^era / D
D      = 1 + log2(1 + miners/1000)
rwa    = 2 × unit × size × duration × compute
size     = 1 + log2(1 + bytes/256KB)
duration = 1 + min(4, pages/10)
compute  = doc 1 · image 1.5 · deed 2 · finance 2.5
caps: 25 AGIS/job · 100 AGIS/wallet/day

On-page numbers are estimates; the claim server pays from your signed claim and the on-chain schedule.
