Roadmap

What works, what we are building, and what comes later. We update this page as things ship.

Now — building

  • Seal + Solana Memo · live
    Fingerprint a file; you (the sealer) pay the tiny SOL fee and sign the receipt.
  • Mine rewards · live
    Run the miner, earn AGIS for verified work.
  • Network attestation signature · in progress
    AGIS signs each proof card (Ed25519) so anyone can check it in the browser. Not eIDAS.
  • Stake AGIS · building
    Stake for miner and API access. Not live yet.
  • Burn AGIS · building
    Paid Verify and fees burn AGIS. Not live yet.

Next — after the pool

  • LP on Raydium · planned
    First AGIS/SOL liquidity pool. No pool exists today.
  • Jupiter / DexScreener · planned
    Routing and charts after the pool exists.
  • Pay in AGIS → swap → burn for anchors · planned
    Part swapped to SOL for the fee, the rest burned.
  • Token price discovery · planned
    A market price only appears after LP. AGIS is not listed anywhere yet.

Later

  • Invoice pilot with 1 real firm · later
    Seal invoices and deliveries inside a real business workflow.
  • Legal partner / true RWA title · later
    Ownership claims only with a licensed legal partner.
  • Mobile apps · later
  • L3 durable index · later
    Long-term storage of proofs beyond our servers.
  • QTSP / notary partner · later
    Qualified signatures only through a licensed partner — we will not imitate them.
  • On-chain site hosting · later
