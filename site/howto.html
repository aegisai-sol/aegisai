Seal vs Verify (simple)

Seal is the RWA add-on of AGIS: real-world files (invoices, receipts, photos) sealed as fingerprint proofs — same AGIS token, not a separate coin.

Seal = create a stamp

Use when you want proof a file looked a certain way at a certain time.

  1. Open Seal.
  2. Pick a file.
  3. Tap Create seal — enough for a fingerprint stamp (simple by default).
  4. Optional: Encrypted backup ⚠ (off by default), validity, or Advanced (passphrase Extra lock, wallet recovery).
  5. Save Seal ID; download recovery kit if you have a key.

After ~20s status becomes verified. L4 decentralized storage is preparing on the Roadmap — not a Seal option yet.

Verify = check a stamp

Use when you (or a boss/friend) want to check an existing stamp.

  1. Open Verify / Proof.
  2. Paste the Seal ID, open the proof link, or upload the same file again.
  3. See: verified or not, expiry date, and the Solana receipt.

Nobody sees the file contents — only the stamp status.

Quick rule
“Stamp this file now”→ Seal
“Is this stamp still good?”→ Verify

Honest note: this proves the file’s fingerprint at time T. It is not a government notary.

Real life — why AGIS

  • Invoice before audit — seal now; auditor later uploads → Verify match. We never read the file.
  • Rental photos — stamp move-in; fingerprint settles “what it looked like”.
  • Contract / receipt — Solana stamp outlives our site; optional passphrase Extra lock on your phone.

Why originality holds — tamper breaks the seal

Original file
SHA-256 9f3a…c71e
✔
Verified · Solana receipt
File with 1 byte changed
SHA-256 4b08…e2d9
✖
No match · tampered
  1. A fingerprint, forever. When you Seal, AGIS records a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the exact file at that moment on Solana. It can’t be edited or deleted.
  2. Change 1 pixel → new fingerprint. Edit even one byte and the fingerprint is completely different, so Verify finds no match. The old seal still proves what the original was.
  3. Recovery needs the same file. Re-uploading finds your seal only with the untouched original — an edited copy never “passes”. That’s a feature.

Why AGIS is the better choice

  • Private by default — optional encryption happens in your browser.
  • Nobody reads your file — miners, servers and the house miner only see ciphertext and the fingerprint, never your photo or receipt.
  • No human reviewer, no AI judgement — no person looks at your document and no AI rewrites or scores it. The network only checks integrity (fingerprint match).
  • Anyone can check — share a proof link; they see status, expiry and the Solana receipt.

Honest note: this is a network proof that the fingerprint was recorded at time T and is unchanged — not a government notary or eIDAS stamp.

How to use AGIS

Three things: mining, sealing a document, and proving it even if you lose your phone.

1 · Mine

  1. Install Phantom (or Solflare / Trust).
  2. Open Miner → Connect Wallet → Start.
  3. Press Claim — AGIS arrives in your wallet.

Beta: no trading (LP/DEX) yet. Not financial advice.

2 · Seal a document

  1. Open Seal → choose a file or camera (up to 20 MB).
  2. Tap Create seal. Optional: Encrypted backup ⚠, validity window, Advanced (passphrase Extra lock / Phantom wallet recovery / remember key).
  3. You get a Seal ID (+ key once if backup on). Free seal = fingerprint on device; tap Put on Solana for a permanent Memo (tiny SOL fee). AGIS pay after liquidity.

3 · Lost phone?

If you still have the file: Verify finds the seal by fingerprint — no key needed. Key / passphrase / wallet unlock only opens an encrypted backup. Download the recovery kit after seal.

In short

Same file → prove the seal without a key. Key only to open encrypted bytes. Passphrase Extra lock is optional. L4 store is preparing — not in the Seal form yet.

RWA add-on: what AGIS proves today

We studied how leading real-world-asset projects (Ondo, Centrifuge, Maple, Backed, Securitize, Figure/Provenance) earn trust, and applied what fits a proof layer:

LayerAGIS todayLater
Proof (file existed, unchanged)Live — fingerprint seal, optional Solana Memo—
Ownership / legal titleNot offeredOnly with a licensed legal partner
YieldNone — seals pay no yieldNot planned without regulation

Practices we follow

  • Separate proof from ownership (like Ondo/Securitize keep tokens and legal structure apart): a seal never claims who owns an asset.
  • Public, checkable receipts (like Centrifuge’s on-chain audit trail): every anchored seal links to its Solana transaction on Solscan.
  • Issuer pays the cost: the person sealing pays the Solana fee (~0.000005 SOL). AegisAI does not pay fees for public seals.
  • Validity windows (like bond maturity): each seal can carry a “valid until” date; expired seals are shown as expired, never silently green.
  • Clear status, no false green: pending, verified, failed and expired are shown separately.
  • Token utility, no overclaim: pay with SOL now; after AGIS has liquidity you can pay with AGIS — part is swapped to SOL for the fee, the rest is burned. There is no AGIS liquidity pool yet.

Honest note: AGIS seals are integrity proofs, not securities, not legal title, not eIDAS or notary stamps.
