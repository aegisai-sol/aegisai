    How Proof-of-Verification works

    01 · Task

    Coordinator issues micro-tasks: verify a claim, check arithmetic, flag hallucinations.

    02 · Verify

    Node runs a tiny check (rules + <1 GB quantized model) in milliseconds on CPU.

    03 · Commit

    Commit–reveal hides votes until the window closes; batch roots are anchored on Solana.

    04 · Reward

    Stake × reputation weighted consensus. Hidden challenge tasks catch lazy or colluding nodes.

    Two sides of one network

    Supply: anyone with a device. Demand: anyone shipping AI.

    Verification market

    For builders & enterprises: send AI output, receive per-claim verdicts plus an on-chain receipt your users and auditors can check. (Verification API — Phase 4.)

    Weak-machine mining

    For node operators: a phone in a browser, a laptop, a Raspberry Pi or a server. No GPU, no hash waste — every vote reduces AI error.

    Reputation staking

    Stake AGIS to weight your votes; accuracy history is public and on-chain.

    Not PoW

    Work produces verified facts, not discarded hashes.

    Not just PoS

    Stake secures the vote; rewards require real verification.

    Not a central fact-checker

    Open network, public receipts, no single judge.

    Honest status: the MVP simulates verification today; small on-device models (<1 GB ONNX) come in Phase 2. Phases & threat model →
