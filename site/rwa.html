Check a file

Someone sent you a file, or you sealed it yourself? Drop it here. We recompute the fingerprint in your browser and look for the seal directly on Solana — no AegisAI server.

Help — where does my data live?
  • Your file — only on your device. It is never uploaded, not even for Verify.
  • Fingerprint + time — on Solana, public and permanent. A fingerprint cannot be turned back into your file.
  • Receipt — you download it. Anyone can check it on Solana.
  • Our servers — store nothing about you or your files.
  • Why a wallet? Solana only accepts records signed by a wallet. You sign, and you pay the tiny network fee (about $0.001) — directly to Solana.
  • What a seal proves: this exact file existed unchanged at that time. It does not prove who wrote it, and it is not a notary or eIDAS stamp.
